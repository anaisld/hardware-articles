About this portfolio

I want to understand how systems behave — and prove it on the bench.

My focus is hands-on engineering: electronics, circuit and system simulation, instrumentation, RF, embedded hardware and control. I enjoy moving between theoretical models, CAD tools, physical assemblies and experimental measurements — especially when the results challenge the initial assumptions.
