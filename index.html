My interests span electronic engineering, embedded systems, RF, power conversion, control engineering, instrumentation, mechatronics, modeling and AI. These projects document how engineering concepts can be translated into circuit models, tested through simulation and evaluated against practical constraints.

Each article focuses on the design choices, governing physical principles, tools, results and limitations of a project.
